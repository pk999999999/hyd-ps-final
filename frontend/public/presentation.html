HYDERABAD GROWTH DESK • EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

HYD-PS2: Hyper-Local AI Growth Desk & Client Portal

AI-assisted business discovery, multi-agent proposal synthesis, and human-in-the-loop relationship building.

The Problem

Cold Outreach to Local Businesses is Broken

  • Stale, Generic Lead Lists: Buying outdated directory dumps leads to high bounce rates and immediate spam reporting.
  • 6+ Hours of Daily Manual Scraping: Operators manually copy-pasting addresses and numbers from Google Maps.
  • Irrelevant, Hallucinated Pitches: Autonomous AI bots sending hallucinated claims without human verification, destroying credibility.
The Solution

Hyper-Localized Intelligence with Human Approval

  • Live Geolocation + Tavily Maps Engine: Instantly finds top 15–20 active businesses using real GPS coordinates and Google Maps citations in seconds.
  • LangGraph Multi-Agent Architecture: Research → Scout → Critic agents formulate concrete, contextual service ideas.
  • Human Checkpoint Guarantee: Every outreach is reviewed, edited, and approved. Zero autonomous spam.

INNOVATION & DATA ENGINE

Real-Time Discovery: No Scrapers, Pure Intelligence

Powered by browser geolocation, OpenStreetMap geocoding, and Tavily's advanced Google Maps entity engine.

The 2-Minute Discovery Workflow

1

Browser Live Location

One-click GPS coordinate capture (±15m accuracy) + Reverse-geocoding into specific localities (e.g., Banjara Hills, Ameerpet).

2

Two Intent Inputs

1. "What's your service?" (e.g. 24/7 Voice Receptionist)
2. "Who are your clients?" (e.g. Hair Salons & Spas)

3

Tavily & Google Maps Search

Advanced semantic search queries citations, filtering out forums and noise, retrieving 15–20 high-fidelity business entities.

Rich Structured Entity Data

  • Verified Business Profile: Real business title, category, physical address with pin, and website.
  • Direct Contact Details: One-click dial phone numbers (+91...) and verified public email.
  • Social Proof & Reputation: Google review star rating (★ 4.5) and review volume count.
  • Direct Navigation: Instant "Open in Google Maps" links with place IDs.
  • Personalized Pitch Generation: Tailored proposition matching the operator's service to the target's operating model.
  • Instant Pipeline Ingestion: Discovered leads automatically sync to PostgreSQL database with deduplication.
💡 Key Judge Takeaway: We eliminated brittle Puppeteer/Selenium scrapers that break on UI updates. Tavily provides resilient, API-first search that scales cleanly in production.

AGENTIC SYSTEM & GOVERNANCE

Multi-Agent LangGraph Pipeline & Human Checkpoints

Deterministic state machines with PostgreSQL checkpoints and few-shot in-context learning.

Phase 1

Scout & Critic Agents

  • Research Node: Evaluates digital footprint, citations, and reviews.
  • Scout Agent: Synthesizes specific bottleneck hypotheses & value hooks.
  • Critic Agent: Audits pitch tone, verifies feasibility, strips pushy jargon.
Phase 2

Durable Human Interrupt

  • LangGraph Checkpoint: Execution interrupts before send; state saved in PostgreSQL.
  • Operator Decision: Approve, edit copy, or reject proposal.
  • Safety & Compliance: AI disclosure, HMAC-signed one-click opt-out, permanent suppression list.
Phase 3

Self-Correcting Memory

  • In-Context Learning: Operator feedback & diffs stored in MongoDB memory.
  • Few-Shot Demonstrations: Past decisions injected into prompt context.
  • Zero Retraining Cost: Adapts tone dynamically without fine-tuning risks.
💡 Key Judge Takeaway: The human checkpoint guarantees safety. PostgreSQL durable checkpoints ensure that workflows survive system restarts without losing state.

FULL-STACK EXECUTION & ROADMAP

Full-Stack Ecosystem: Voice Agents & Client Portal

End-to-end integration connecting initial discovery directly to signed client intake.

Integrated Client Experience

  • 7-Day Signed Client Invitations: One-click secure tokenized link sent to prospective business.
  • Dedicated Client Portal: Business owner accesses a clean dashboard to review recommendations, request appointments, or chat.
  • ElevenLabs Conversational Voice: WebRTC conversational voice assistant for both operator pipeline analysis and client service intake.
  • Multi-Channel Dispatch: Email via Resend with audit trails + SMS gateway via Textbee Android integration.

Production Technology Stack

  • Frontend: Next.js 16 (Turbopack), React 19, TypeScript, Phosphor Icons, Responsive CSS.
  • Backend API: FastAPI (Async/Await), Python 3.13, Uvicorn, Alembic.
  • Data & Storage: PostgreSQL 16 (Relational + Graph Checkpoints), MongoDB (Memory & Chat), Redis (Pub/Sub & WebSocket).
  • AI Models: OpenRouter (LLM Reasoning), Tavily API (Search & Maps), ElevenLabs (Voice).
